    Getting around

    • Sri Lanka, 23 Nov – 5 Dec: a private car and driver (Lion Cab & Tours), from the 02:45 airport pickup to the airport drop on 5 Dec.
    • 1 Dec: the scenic train from Ella to Nanu Oya, first class. The driver takes the bags ahead and meets us at Nanu Oya.

      Good to know

      • Sri Lanka needs an ETA (electronic travel authorisation) for each of us before landing.
      • Many sights sell tickets at the gate: Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa, the Sigiriya water gardens and Pinnawala.
      • The VietJet allowance is 20 kg per bag. Weigh the bags on the night of 4 Dec.
      • The Maldives green tax is paid at the Hulhumalé hotel on check-in.
      • Carry a copy of the wedding card for the resort's honeymoon cake.
      • 24 Nov is a Poya (full-moon) day: no alcohol is sold in Sri Lanka.
