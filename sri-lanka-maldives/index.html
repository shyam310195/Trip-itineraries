Kratisha & Shyam · 21 November – 10 December 2026

Sri Lanka → Maldives

Twenty days, two travellers. Surf towns and whale boats on the south coast, leopards at Yala, the tea-country train, Kandy's temple at dusk, the ancient cities, then a lagoon for an anniversary and a birthday.

The plan

Full days, one loop, then the sea

We land in Colombo in the small hours of 23 November and head straight down to Weligama. From there the road loops east to Yala, climbs into the hills at Ella, takes the train through the tea estates, and drops to Kandy and the Cultural Triangle before ending in Colombo.

On 5 December we fly to Malé for a night in Hulhumalé, three nights in an overwater villa, and one more night by the airport before flying home to Bengaluru.

Everything goes by road with a private driver, except one scenic train ride. No big climbs.

    Good to know

    • Sri Lanka needs an ETA (electronic travel authorisation) for each of us before landing.
    • Many sights sell tickets at the gate: Temple of the Tooth, Peradeniya, Dambulla, Polonnaruwa, the Sigiriya water gardens and Pinnawala.
    • The VietJet allowance is 20 kg per bag. Weigh the bags on the night of 4 Dec.
    • The Maldives green tax is paid at the Hulhumalé hotel on check-in.
    • Carry a copy of the wedding card for the resort's honeymoon cake.
